## Trout Lake oak

**Ammatelli, Joseph; Ciruzzi, Dominick; Loheide II, Steven; Lundquist, Jessica
(2023). “Video and accelerometer tree sway data for an oak tree in Trout Lake,
Wisconsin.” Zenodo. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.7988211.**

- Source and metadata: https://zenodo.org/records/7988211
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original source: `trout-c.MP4`; bundled `trout-c.mp4` resized to 640 × 360,
  converted to H.264 at 24 fps, audio omitted. Poster and sampled frames are
  derivatives. Original checksum and metadata are recorded in `docs/data`.
- The camera was mounted on an adjacent tree. The preset reference is
  provisional; source provenance forces a recapture result.
- Published optical estimates are 0.23–0.24 Hz. The matching 60-second sensor
  estimate is 0.49 Hz; the 30-minute sensor result is 0.24 Hz. These are different
  methods/windows. ArborPulse does not claim agreement or sensor validation.
